Privacy policy

This policy covers NEON HEAT: Drift & Chase for iPhone and iPad, published by YTPAVLOV (Himmet Atakan Dogan). Contact: ytpavloviletisim@gmail.com.

Local progress and optional sign-in

Guest play is available. Career progress, cars, coins, preferences and reward delivery records are stored on your device. A locally stored Apple or Google account identifier selects your driver profile. A display name returned by sign-in is used in memory. The publisher does not operate a career server or provide cross-device save synchronization.

Apple and Google process optional sign-in through their services. Google sign-in requests the provider's basic profile and account information, including data normally returned by its sign-in SDK. The game uses the stable account identifier and given name; it does not ask for contacts or access to your email messages. Authentication tokens are managed by the provider SDK. You may use guest mode instead.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for ads and regional privacy choices. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may transmit IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, performance and diagnostic information to Google and its advertising partners to deliver, measure and protect ads. This third-party processing can occur even for non-personalized advertising. We configure ad personalization and publisher first-party ID off and do not request the iOS tracking permission or precise location.

Where required, a privacy consent form appears before ad requests. You can reopen available privacy choices in the game settings. If ads cannot be requested, gameplay remains available and unavailable ad rewards are not granted. Optional rewarded videos grant a benefit only after completion is confirmed. Interstitial ads appear at eligible session breaks.

Read Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising information for its processing, retention and controls. Third-party retention follows the provider's policies.

Purchases

Apple processes optional in-app coin purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. The app verifies StoreKit purchase transactions and stores transaction identifiers locally to prevent duplicate delivery. Apple processes purchase information under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications, support and permissions

Optional return reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The app does not use a publisher push-notification server. Notification permission is optional and can be disabled in iOS or game settings. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location access.

If you contact support, the publisher receives the email and information you voluntarily include to address your request. Avoid sending passwords, full payment information or unrelated personal information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion.

Deletion and your choices

Settings → Delete profile removes the current locally stored career and its backup. Signing out disconnects the active local profile. Google authorization can also be revoked in your Google account, and Sign in with Apple access can be removed in Apple account settings. Deleting the app removes its app-container progress; iOS Keychain records may remain until cleared by iOS. Contact the relevant provider for data it processes. Contact the publisher for access, correction or deletion requests concerning support correspondence.

Audience and policy changes

This is a general-audience arcade driving game, not a children's account service. It does not include player chat, public user profiles, user-generated content or a publisher analytics SDK. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support. If you believe a child has submitted such information, contact us to request removal.

We may update this policy when the game or services change. The date above identifies the current version.
