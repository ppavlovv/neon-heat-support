PIT STOP / PLAYER SUPPORT

Need a hand?

Contact ytpavloviletisim@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. Never send your password or payment card details.

Where is my career saved?

Your career is stored on this device. Signing in selects your local driver profile; it does not sync progress between devices. Keep the app installed to retain progress.

How do ad rewards work?

Choose an optional rewarded video for a second chance after capture or to double session winnings. A reward is granted only after the ad service confirms completion. Closing an ad early does not grant a reward. An ad may be unavailable.

How do purchases work?

The game is free. Optional coin packs use Apple's purchase system and the price shown by the App Store. All cars and upgrades can be earned by playing. Coins are consumable items; spent coins cannot be restored. For a missing completed purchase, contact support with the transaction reference, never your card details.

How do I delete a profile or turn off reminders?

Open Settings in the game to delete the current local profile, change notification preferences, or sign out. You can also disable notifications in iOS Settings. Profile deletion is permanent and removes its local career.
